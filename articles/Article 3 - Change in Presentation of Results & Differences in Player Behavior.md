The question is "Would people choose to bet on the jackpot more if they saw it last longer in the game?"

To understand this I created a roulette style game and set out to see if I could get people to bet worse if I changed how the game revealed the winning tile.

29 Participants were used for this study and were sourced in person and virtually. All participants were active students at DigiPen Institute of Technology.

### Methods
Both groups have the same exact odds of winning each individual tile on the board.
In Group A tiles that were more valuable, Diamonds and Stars would disappear first.
In group B tiles that were less valuable, Circles and Triangles would disappear first.
Participants would choose a bet size and a type of tile to bet on.
Tiles would then disappear until a single tile remained.
If that tile matched the one that the player bet on then the player would win a multiplier of their bet chips based on what tile they bet on.
Each participant played 5 rounds.

### Results
An independent sample t-test was used to check for significance in participants’ frequency of betting on the star and which group they were in As well as participants’ average bet size and which group they were in.

- No significant difference between when the valuable tiles were removed and how frequently they bet on the jackpot tile. Early Removal of Valuable Tiles (M = 0.733, SD = 0.961) and Late Removal of Valuable Tiles (M = 1.2, SD = 1.373), t(28) = -1.078, p > .05, 95% CI [-1.353, 0.42]. d = -0.394
- There was a significant difference between when the valuable tiles were removed and how much they bet on average. Early Removal of Valuable Tiles  (M = 1559, SD = 603.1) and Late Removal of Valuable Tiles (M = 2289, SD = 1053).
- Those that had Late Removal of the more valuable tiles bet more on average throughout the whole play session. t(27) = -2.664, p < .05, 95% CI [-1524, -197.9], d = -0.859.

### Why Does this Matter?
This study shows how powerful we can be as designers. Without changing the odds of the game we've seen shifts in player's betting habits. Being thoughtful and "cheating" in this way can be a powerful tool to make games more exciting for players to engage with.
