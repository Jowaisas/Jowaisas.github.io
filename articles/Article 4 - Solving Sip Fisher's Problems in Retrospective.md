## The Game Did Well
As of writing this the game sits with a 92% positive review score on steam this is to say that while I am going to be focusing on the problems 

## One Very Evil Fish
While am not sure about the scale of this issue we certainly got a couple of review about it. Some people had a lot of difficulty getting the final fish at the end of the game. The tricky thing is that while they have certainly gotten fish earlier on none of those fish were required while the final fish of the game is required. 

There is nothing wrong with ending the game with a set challenge the player must complete but I think the previous 4 hours of their experience followed a different set of rules. Losing a fish usually just means some lost money nothing too big, but at the end it meant being unable to complete the gain 

#### The Fix
Having fish slowly tire out and become easier to sip up over time would have likely gone unnoticed and would have allowed all players to complete the game. Sip Fisher is designed to be a cozy experience so maintaining the challenge of the final boss battle isn't the priority. 

## Missing Journal Entries
Missing a Jumbo Fish can be a devastating experience for a completionist. Fish appear randomly and sometimes if a player is efficient they can leave a zone before catching all of the available fish. The issue is that once a player has realized this the only way to be able to catch that fish again is to rewind your progress. 

The issue here is that it feels bad to rewind your progress. A couple ideas we're thought of to fix this but we didn't have the development time to implement any of them.

#### The Fix
The idea would be to have a pond hidden near the end of the game that would never go down as you sipped it. It would also contain every single fish in the game so that if you missed a fish you could just sit in this location and eventually find it.
