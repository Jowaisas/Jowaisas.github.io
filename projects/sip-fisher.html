Sip Fisher

Incremental Fishing Game

Sip Fisher is a cozy incremental fishing game that me and 7 others started in late April 2025 and released to steam in late August 2025.

This was my first shipped game! I learned a lot about having a bit of the public eye on you and getting flooded with feedback.

Details

  • Platforms: PC (Steam), Nintendo Switch
  • Development Time: 4 months
  • Engine: Unity
  • Team: Syphon Software (8 People)
