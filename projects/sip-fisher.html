Sip Fisher

Incremental Fishing Game

Sip Fisher is a cozy incremental fishing game where you drink up a lake and all of it's fish through a straw.

My Experience

This was my first shipped game! Despite being in control this project really felt like its deadlines were so real compared to other things I've worked on in the past for school. We did Steam's Next Fest and Fishing Fest both of these being important milestones for the game.

This game also managed to have some popularity of Youtube which was a very surreal experience to see so many people playing my game.
